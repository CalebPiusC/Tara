# Speaker Identification Research
Summary of [research/DIARIZATION_OPTIONS.md](DIARIZATION_OPTIONS.md). Speaker *change/cluster detection* ≠ *person identification*. Best evidence is platform per-participant audio; diarization on mixed audio is anonymous and unstable; voice enrollment raises biometric privacy issues and needs consent. PoC: fuse platform hints + diarization + self-introduction; below threshold → `unknown`. Limitations: overlap, short utterances, similar voices, label instability. No claim of accuracy; measure false-attribution rate (see [TESTING_STRATEGY.md](../getting-started/TESTING_STRATEGY.md)).
